# Actual UI

Use original captures or an approved local/demo app. Verify features, wording and current product positioning before writing claims. Prefer dummy data; avoid names, tokens, account menus/customer content unless authorized. Never put private captures in a reusable global skill.

Show the path to the feature, then the action. A visible click gets a lead-in and settle; capture menus/cards opening in the actual app. Scroll list contents, show actual toggles/timeline movement. A screenshot can demonstrate cropping/annotation, not a backend operation: label it as a screenshot study rather than faking live interaction.

For signed-in apps use permitted browser controls. Access does not authorize changing settings, credit spending, exports or publication. A deterministic local demo can be used when authorized; isolate its data/port and avoid production APIs. The bundled capture tool serves a scoped local project, not login sessions.

Keep source screenshots unchanged. Define image-space crop rectangles and a continuous eased camera path; cursor/highlights/labels follow the same transform. Short external labels and leader lines should not cover fields. Distinguish generated, retained and human values with words/shapes as well as colour where relevant. Hold on readable text; don't oscillate zoom just to fill time. Check encoded pixels at phone size.

Do not invent UI/data for attractive numbers. Record editorial pointers/transitions in provenance. Inventory sources and end cards on revision so old product positioning cannot survive accidentally. Portrait usually needs new crops and label placement.
