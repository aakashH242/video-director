Before copying third-party code/assets inspect current licences and retain notices. Three.js/fonts remain project dependencies with their own licences. No exclusive ownership claim over upstream techniques.

Lessons: inventory revised end cards; show real clicks/toggles/scrolls; continuous camera/phase wraps; correct fade material/depth flags; mark parts as named; inspect encoded frames; audition voices instead of trusting technical success. These are useful decision criteria, not a mandatory visual style or list of one-off bans.
